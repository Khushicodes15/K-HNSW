# Empirical Claims Register (`docs/claims.md`)

> **Scope**: All empirical claims, methodological assertions, and hypotheses across repository documentation (`docs/`), benchmark scripts (`scripts/`), and committed JSON results (`docs/data/`).  
> **Status Taxonomy**:  
> - `supported`: Empirically verified across multiple random seeds in a valid, committed JSON benchmark.  
> - `unsupported`: Lacks empirical backing or invalidated by subsequent controlled experiments.  
> - `withdrawn`: Formally retracted due to invalid assumptions, harness bugs, or failure to meet pre-registered thresholds.  
> - `radial-protocol-only`: Holds strictly under the non-generalizable PRNG radial-drift artifact ($\cos \equiv 1.0$); fails under realistic isotropic drift.  
> - `untested hypothesis`: Plausible theoretical explanation that lacks direct microscopic verification.
